---
name: notes
description: Use when the user refers to their notes or to Saturn (a note by name, the note they have open, their tasks or to-dos, a space, board or canvas), or asks to save, file, capture or find something in their notes.
---

Saturn is the user's notes app on this Mac, and the `saturn` tools reach it. Use them when the user:

- mentions their notes, a note by its title, or the note open in Saturn
- wants something kept: an idea, a summary of this conversation, a link, a task
- asks about their tasks, to-dos or due dates
- names a space, a board column or a canvas

Files in the current folder or project are not Saturn notes. Read those with the ordinary file tools.

When no `saturn` tools are available (Claude on the web or a phone, or Saturn not set up on this Mac), say that Saturn's notes are reachable only from Claude on the Mac where Saturn runs, and don't guess what the notes say.
